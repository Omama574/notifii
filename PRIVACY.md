# Notifii — Privacy Policy (draft)

_Last updated: 12 November 2026. Replace the bracketed parts before publishing._

**Short version:** Notifii can't send anything anywhere. It has no internet permission, no account, and no analytics.

## What Notifii reads
With your permission (Android "Notification access"), Notifii reads incoming notifications so it can sort them onto your ladder (Siren, Ring, Later, Hide).

## Where it's stored
Notifications are saved in the Vault in Notifii's private storage on your device, for as long as you choose (30 days, 90 days, or forever). The Vault is excluded from Google cloud backup. Notifii has no servers and the developer never receives your data.

## Internet
Notifii doesn't declare Android's `INTERNET` permission, so the system itself blocks it from going online. You can check this in the app's permissions on your phone.

## Purchases
Notifii Pro is processed by Google Play through the Play Store app on your phone. Notifii only receives confirmation that you own it, never your payment details.

## Your control
- Empty the Vault any time in Settings.
- Turn off Notification access in Android settings at any time.
- Uninstalling Notifii deletes everything it stored.
